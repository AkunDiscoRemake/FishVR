Controls

  • Trigger: hold & release (or swing) to cast, strike on a bite, press on each PERFECT! ring during the fight.
  • Touchpad / stick: up/down walk, left/right turn. Centre click: crouch. Hold centre: menu.
  • Desktop: WASD walk, Q/E turn, right-drag or click-lock to look, left click / Space = trigger, C crouch, M menu.
  • Cardboard: tap screen = trigger, gaze at buttons and tap. Look down + hold to walk.
